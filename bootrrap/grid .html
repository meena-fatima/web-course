Next Planner's Academy

Next Planners Institute of Future Technologies (also known as Next Planner Academy) in Skardu is a growing educational center. It helps local youth learn modern tech and practical skills.The academy is located near Abdullah Hospital in Skardu. It offers short courses in areas like web development, graphic design, digital marketing, machine learning, and python programming. The school focuses on hands-on learning. Students go on field trips and join career counseling events to get ready for jobs in the digital world. Their goal is to give students the tools they need to succeed in tech and help the local community grow.
